Email accounts

Add every inbox that gets job alerts or replies from employers. Each needs its own app password.

How to get a Gmail app password
  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification at myaccount.google.com/security.
  2. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords, name it “Dispatch”, and copy the 16-character code.
  3. In Gmail, Settings → See all settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP → enable IMAP.

Passwords stay on this computer in data/settings.json. Dispatch reads mail without marking it read, and only sends alerts to you.
